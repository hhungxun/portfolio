This is an unpublished working draft, not a peer-reviewed result. The present
claim is deliberately narrow: classical localisation regions can organize the
eigenvalue geometry of the Weyl matrix, and suggest a scalar measure of spectral
asymmetry. The invariant and its interpretation remain to be checked case by
case.
