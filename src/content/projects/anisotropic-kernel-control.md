The manuscript formulates discrete optimisation on $(SO(3))^N$ and a continuous
geometric-control problem. Its holonomy check at $\theta_0=\pi/4$ agrees with
the Gauss--Bonnet prediction, but conjectural statements remain unproved and
some numerical experiments are still marked TBD. It has not been submitted.
